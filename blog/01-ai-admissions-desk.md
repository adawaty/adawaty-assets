## What is an AI admissions assistant?

**An AI admissions assistant is software that answers prospective students and parents instantly, using your school's own information, and hands every conversation to your admissions team as a lead.**

It is not a generic chatbot. It is trained on your fees, programs, deadlines, documents and policies. It replies in the channel the family already uses. And it knows when to pass a conversation to a human.

## Why do schools lose applicants before the first reply?

Families shop around. They message several schools at once and often go with whoever answers clearly first. Your admissions team is excellent, but it works office hours and handles one call at a time.

The cost is hard to see because it never shows up as a "no". It shows up as silence:

- An unanswered WhatsApp message on a Friday night.
- A missed call during a busy open day.
- A web form that sits in a shared inbox until Monday.

**The fix is not more staff. It is a first reply that never sleeps.**
