## What is an online academy?

**An online academy is a branded website where students can buy, take and complete your courses, with payments, progress tracking and certificates in one place.**

It is different from uploading videos to a marketplace. You own the brand, the domain, the student list and the pricing.

## Why do most course launches stall?

Teams usually stall in the same three places:

- **Tool overload.** A video host, a payment tool, a website builder and a certificate app that do not talk to each other.
- **Perfect-course syndrome.** Waiting to record ten courses before selling one.
- **No reason to finish.** Students start, drift and never come back.

**The fix is one flagship course, live and selling, before you build the second.**
